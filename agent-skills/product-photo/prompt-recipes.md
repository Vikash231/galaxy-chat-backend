# Prompt recipes

## White studio (catalog / marketplace)
Product photo of {product} on a seamless pure white background, soft even studio lighting, subtle natural shadow beneath, centred, sharp focus, no props.

## Coloured backdrop
Product photo of {product} on a solid {colour} backdrop, soft diffused light from the left, gentle shadow, minimal, centred composition.

## Lifestyle scene
{product} in a {scene, e.g. sunlit kitchen counter}, natural window light, shallow depth of field, realistic, the product is the clear focus.

## Flat lay
Top-down flat lay of {product} on {surface}, arranged with a few related items, even soft light, clean spacing.

## Hero / premium
{product} on a dark reflective surface, dramatic rim lighting, subtle reflection, premium advertising style.
